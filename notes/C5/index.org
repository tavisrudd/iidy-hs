Chosen: =pandoc -f gfm-tex_math_dollars-tex_math_gfm -t org -s --wrap=none
--shift-heading-level-by=-1=, then post-processing: drop the
=:PROPERTIES:/:CUSTOM_ID:= drawers; rewrite =x.md= links to =[[file:x.org]]=;
protect the em and en dash characters (pandoc rewrites them as =---= and
=--=); add =#+options: ^:nil -:nil= under =#+title:= (without it =ERR_3001=
renders as a subscript); merge adjacent verbatim spans joined by a slash, and a span plus a slash-joined word, into one verbatim
span because a slash touching a span breaks Org emphasis. The gfm
=tex_math_dollars= extension had to be disabled: it ate text between two
=$= in prose. Spans containing an equals sign or edge whitespace use =~...~= or have the
whitespace moved outside (a handful of lines). Heading links use
=[[*Heading]]= (same file) and =[[file:x.org::*Heading]]=; no =id:= stamps.
=docs/README.org= converted too. Code blocks keep their language
(=pseudocode= stays as =#+begin_src pseudocode=). Scripts: =c peek= resolves
the heading form (see last task).
