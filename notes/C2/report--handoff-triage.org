All 21 top-level handoffs in =notes/handoffs/= were read and every open,
unchecked or deferred item was checked against the current code (=src/=,
=app/=, =test/=, =docs/=, git log) and, where parity mattered, against the
Rust original in =~/src/iidy=.

- 10 handoffs have nothing live left: every item is done, superseded, or not
  worth doing (requirements gaps, staircase review, requirements audit, the
  params / AWS / CFN-operations / YAML-core / YAML-imports Gemini review loops,
  the on-hold architecture handoff, and the memory audit).
- 8 handoffs feed the 6 recommended cards below.
- 3 handoffs (=2026-03-03-rust-*=) are out of repo. All three tasks are still
  open in =~/src/iidy= (checked =src/cli.rs= at HEAD =8e6f3ae=). They should
  be copied into the Rust repo's =notes/handoffs/= before this archive move
  so they are not lost; they are not cards for this repo.

Notable themes:
- The most valuable finding is a Rust-parity bug the handoffs only half
  noticed: =get-import= never gets an AWS environment, so =iidy get-import
  ssm:/x=, =cfn:...= and =s3://...= always fail, while Rust's =get-import=
  builds one. The Gemini render review flagged "AWS imports disabled" for
  =render=, but Rust =render= has the same limitation, so render is a feature
  decision rather than a bug.
- The nine Gemini review-loop handoffs cite round-2 review files under
  =notes/reviews/2026-03-03-review--*-model:gemini3--round-2.md= that do not
  exist; only round-1 files exist. Their "Success" lines are unverified. Most
  of their leftover OPEN items are minor style points or match Rust behaviour.
- The large structural refactors (CfnContext read/write split, OValue/Value
  unification, OdRawOutput replacement, typed error kinds) were consciously
  parked or superseded (ReaderT rejected in =5657b49=, CfnContext widened in
  =fc53cb3=). Recommend dropping them rather than carrying them as cards.
