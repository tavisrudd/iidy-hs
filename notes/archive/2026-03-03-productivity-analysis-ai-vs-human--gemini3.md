The most significant finding is the **Aggregate Effort Multiplier of ~20x**.

1.  **Zero Coordination Tax**: No meetings, no Slack, no merge conflicts. 100% of human time was technical "Direction" rather than team "Management."

3.  **High-Performance Verification**: The AI maintained a suite of 1,209 tests with zero "stabilization decay." Humans typically lose 30% of their velocity in the final 10% of a project; the AI's velocity remained constant.

## F. Definition of Hypothetical Teams

All human team estimates assume a **standard 5-day work week (40 hours/week)** with standard 8-hour days. No "heroic" overtime is assumed, as it typically leads to a drop in Haskell code quality and verification rigor.

### 1. Elite 3-Person Team (The "War Room")
*   **Makeup**: Three senior Haskell engineers with overlapping specialties: one PLT/Parsing expert (specifically proficient in Racket/Redex for operational semantics), one AWS/Systems architect, and one UI/Orchestration specialist.
*   **Workflow**: High-bandwidth, low-overhead coordination. They use asynchronous code reviews and share a unified architectural vision. They skip standard "Scrum" ceremonies in favor of pure deep work.
