Templates loaded from remote locations are not allowed to use the local-only
import types (=file:=, =env:=, =git:=, =filehash:=, =filehash-base64:=).
The check is =isRemoteBase= in =src/Iidy/Yaml/Imports/Types.hs= (around
lines 81 and 122), which treats a parent as remote only when its location
starts with =s3://=, =http://= or =https://=. A document loaded through the
short form =s3:bucket/key= (=src/Iidy/Yaml/Imports/Loaders/S3.hs= around
lines 38, 66 and 127 keep that spelling as the base), or through =ssm:= or
=cfn:=, therefore counts as local, and a remote template can reach local
files, environment variables and git data by importing one. Found and
confirmed by the C4 review: [[id:org:01a1e68e-1585-707c-91d5-94cf1de05539][High: import trust gate fails open]].
The redesign of what remote templates may do at all is
[[file:../C15/index.org][C15]]; this card is the narrow fix.
