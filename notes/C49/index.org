Found by the C30 re-review (2026-11-17). The demo's =--timescaling=
option is parsed with =auto= and no bound (=src/Iidy/Cli/Parser.hs=, the
=timescaling= option of the demo command). C30 bounded =sleep:= to 0..86400
seconds, but the delay is =sleepMicros secs timescaling=
(=src/Iidy/Demo.hs=), so =--timescaling 2e8= with =sleep: 86400= overflows
=Int= and the step does not sleep at all. Infinity, NaN and negative factors
are accepted; a negative one skips every delay. Rust takes =timescaling= as
an =f64= (=~/src/iidy/src/demo.rs=, check the CLI definition) and casts the
scaled value with =as u64=, which saturates; check what Rust does with a
negative factor before choosing the error text.
